## Original procedural assets

Product geometry for T-shirts, frame-protection kits, flexible mudguards, claw clips and combs; the prototype inspection fixture, label applicator and garage; and generated label graphics are original code in this repository. These are development assets, not final photorealistic models.

Existing walls/terrain and much of the furniture still use original procedural geometry and texture generation. The founder remains procedural, without a licensed humanoid rig. A complete realism/art replacement is unfinished.
